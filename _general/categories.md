# Full-Game Categories

If you're interested in learning a full-game category for SMBW, you're in the right place.

For the Switch 1 Edition, we have *3* main full-game categories you can choose from:

[***Any%***](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=any&x=9kvg5v3k): Beat the game as fast as possible
- The *Overworld Out-of-Bounds* tricks are allowed, which allows for **Worlds 1-3 to be skipped**.
- This means that **multiplayer mode is allowed, but you must be by yourself controlling 2 characters (1P2C)**
- This also makes the category **the shortest out of the main categories**.
- However, **v1.0.0 is REQUIRED to run this category**, which unfortunately makes the category quite inaccessible for most aspiring runners.
- **Fast Dash** reigns supreme in this category, as **Jet Run** is too far out of the way. If you enjoy Fast Dash, this might be the category for you!

[***Any% No Major Glitches (NMG)***](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=any&x=9d8w137d): Beat the game as fast as possible, without the use of major glitches (*Overworld Out-of-Bounds*)
- By far **the most popular and accessible category and is recommended for beginners**.
- Can be played on **any version of SMBW**, so it can be played on Switch 1 or 2.
- The length is **~40m longer than Any%**.
- **Jet Run** is the speed badge of choice in this category, but **Fast Dash** can be used depending on personal preference, with it only being *~40-50s* slower.

[***100%***](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=100-standees&x=xd1x9vrk-onv5r4wl.1py7gmg1): Beat 100% of the game as fast as possible
- 100% requires obtaining **all 6 medals on your save file**, which are obtained by:
	- Completing the main story (finishing ‘Bowsers Rage Stage’).
	- Touching the top of the flagpole at the end of every level.
	- Obtaining every wonder seed.
	- Collecting every ‘10’ flower coin.
	- Getting every badge.
	- Getting every standee.
- By far **the longest category, over 2 and a half hours more than NMG**
- Unfortunately a **very RNG-heavy category** due to Standee luck
	- There is a sub-category for [100% that does not obtain Standees](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=100-no-standees&x=xd1x9vrk-onv5r4wl.qkerxynq) for those that are turned off by RNG.
