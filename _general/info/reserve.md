# Reserve Cancel

In SMBW, you are given the ability to store an additional power-up in your "reserve balloon" that you can spawn at any time by holding the A button for a couple of seconds.

If you switch to a new power-up when already wearing a different power-up, your currently equipped power-up gets added to your reserve. If you already have a power-up in your reserve, whatever is there before is replaced by your currently equipped power-up.

For example, if you have the Elephant form equipped currently, with a Bubble Flower in reserve, and then touch a Drill Mushroom, you will turn into Drill form and the Elephant Fruit will overwrite the Bubble Flower that was in your reserve initially.

However, *there is a hidden trick you can do to change this behavior.* If you ***hold the A button to summon your reserve balloon as you collect a new power-up, your reserved item will stay the same and will NOT be replaced**.* This is referred to as a **Reserve Cancel.**

This adds a new layer to this game's power-up management, as **Reserve Cancels** effectively delete your currently equipped power-up and allows you to equip a new power-up without worrying about overwriting your reserve.

## Any% No Major Glitches

Reserve Cancels are used a total of twice in the highest level route of the **Any% No Major Glitches** category.

The first one is done right after leaving World 3, in **Jewel Block Cave.** You enter the level as Elephant with a Bubble Flower in reserve, and we want to keep this Bubble Flower in reserve as it is essential to saving time from World 4 and onwards. This level serves as an introduction to the Drill Mushroom, and we want to equip it to use for this level and for a few Wonder Seeds in early World 4.

So, we can avoid swapping power-ups unnecessarily by executing a Reserve Cancel upon collecting the first Drill Mushroom of the level, giving us the proper power-up state that we want for the next part of the run.

The second time it is used is for the most optimal cycle in **Search Party: Item Park**. This level requires use of all 3 useful power-ups throughout the run to collect each Wonder Token, but we want to end this level with both Bubble Flower and Drill Mushroom, as Elephant is the least useful out of these for saving time in World 6.

The optimal Item Park cycle starts with obtaining the Elephant Fruit, grabbing the Wonder Token that requires swiping the spike ball with your trunk, and then immediately swapping to Drill using a Reserve Cancel just like in Jewel Block Cave to complete the rest of the level.

## 100%

In the **100%** category, Reserve Cancels are used much more frequently as they can be utilized to snag extra flower coins from collecting power-ups while keeping an important item in reserve.

For instance, if you are currently in Elephant form and you want to keep a Bubble Flower in reserve, you can collect additional Elephant Fruits to obtain 1 flower coin each time, and as long as you are executing a Reserve Cancel, your Bubble Flower will stay and now you are up more flower coins.

It is also important to note that 100% does the same Reserve Cancel in Item Park as Any% NMG for the same reasons as that category, but does NOT do the one in Jewel Block Cave, as Elephant *is* used in World 4 in that category and as such wants to keep it in reserve.
