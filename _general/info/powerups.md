# Power-up Intricacies

As is usually the case for 2D Mario games, the power-ups are what set each game apart from each other, and SMBW is by no means an exception to this. 

There are a total of 3 unique power-ups that you can find throughout the game, and this section is meant to explain some of the intricacies behind these power-ups:
- Elephant Fruit
- Bubble Flower
- Drill Mushroom

It is important to note that while there are only 3 unique power-ups, the Fire Flower returns as the only legacy power-up that you can find in the game, and is by far the least useful out of the 4, having only very niche applications in certain ILs like World 5's KO Arena, so we will *not* be discussing it any further.

There is also a brand-new power-up exclusive to the Switch 2 Edition of the game, which is the Super Flower Pot. This power-up allows you to shoot flowers that move upwards that defeat enemies quickly, and the ability to flutter by holding jump.

## Elephant Fruit

The Elephant Fruit is the first power-up you are meant to obtain, as it is present in the first level, as well as the mascot power-up of the game.

This power-up turns your character into an elephant, becoming taller and wider, along with having the ability to use your trunk to swat enemies or spray water if stored up. It also makes you ground pound faster due to increased

The Elephant Fruit doesn't seem like a super amazing power-up at first glance, but it actually gets quite a decent amount of use. For full-game categories such as Any% No Major Glitches and 100%, the Elephant Fruit gets most of its use in the early game while appearing briefly in later sections.
#### Brick Break Master

While in elephant form, you can break blocks such as turn blocks and brick blocks from below without bonking back down to the ground, going straight through as if they weren't there.

This saves time in some levels such as World 1 Palace that have some of these blocks as obstacles.

#### Trunk Grabs

The most important benefit of the Elephant Fruit is being able to interact with objects with your trunk. Specifically, being able to *grab items earlier than intended.*

This includes items such as single and 10 flower coins, hidden blocks, Wonder Tokens, and even Wonder Seeds!

Most levels that see elephant be used utilize this ability, although it is especially apparently in levels such as [Search Party: Puzzling Park](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw/runs/y9dw9rnm).

## Bubble Flower

The Bubble Flower is first introduced in the level *Blewbird Roost* in Petal Isles shortly before you enter World 2, and it is one of the most overtuned power-ups ever introduced to 2D Mario. It is the most used power-up in every full-game category and sees plenty of use in ILs.

The power-up allows you to shoot bubbles by pressing the run button which can be used to defeat enemies as well as bounce off those bubbles to reach farther distances. You also summon two bubbles when you spin jump with this power-up, similar to the Fire and Ice Flower in other 2D Mario games.
#### Infinite Bubble Jumps

With the Bubble Flower, you are able to infinitely jump on bubbles by timing your bubble shots in a specific way. This is extremely important to master, so important that we decided to [give it a page all to itself](https://notcodek.github.io/TestProjectWonderTut/ibj).

#### Bubble Boosted Jumps

Outside of doing an infinite chain, there is another utilization of bubble jumps that are referred to as **Bubble Boosted Jumps**.

Instead of landing *on* your bubble, you can jump *through* the bubble which extends your jump height.

This is useful for making jumps that are a bit too high to make, without having the space needed to get a bigger bubble jump chain going.

While this technique can be done with any badge, it is much more consistent with Jet Run than with other badges.

## Drill Mushroom

The Drill Mushroom is the last power-up you are intended to see, being found first in *Jewel-Block Cave* in Petal Isles right before the entry to World 4. While it is definitely the least used out of the 2 power-ups, it definitely has it's place in SMBW.

This power-up allows you to drill into certain terrain, replacing your crouch. This allows you to skip over enemies and go through gaps that you can't otherwise. You can also drill upwards into ceilings in the same way, along with defeating enemies that fall on your drill cap.
