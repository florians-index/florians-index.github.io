# Infinite Bubble Jumps

As mentioned in our [power-up explanation page](https://notcodek.github.io/TestProjectWonderTut/powerups), the Bubble Flower power-up is extremely useful for saving time in SMBW. 

Perhaps the most broken ability of the Bubble Flower is the ability to **infinitely chain bubble jumps together to reach unlimited horizontal and vertical distances**. This technique is referred to as a ***Infinite Bubble Jump, or IBJ.***

The premise of inputting an IBJ is very straightforward:
- Spawn a bubble
- Position a jump to land on said bubble
- *Before landing on the bubble*, spawn another bubble
- Land on the original bubble
- Repeat

Doing an IBJ can be easier or harder depending on your equipped badge, and your timing of your second (and beyond) bubble changes how much height you gain for each bubble jump.

The general idea is that the *earlier* you shoot your next bubble, the higher your next bubble jump will go, in exchange for making it slightly more strict to make.

#### How to Practice

The best place to practice IBJs is in **Jump! Jump! Jump!** as there is a wide open space very fit for this technique.

![]({{ '/assets/images/info/ibjprac.png' | relative_url }})

It is important to be aware of "bubble RNG" when practicing these, as exact bubble height and speed are not *exactly* in your control, but bad bubble RNG is not typically lethal if you react accordingly.

With no badge or Fast Dash, doing a twirl with ZR/R during your IBJ can be useful to maintain horizontal distance in case you shoot your bubble too high or it goes too fast.

Jet Run IBJs can be pretty tricky to get the hang of, especially if you're used to doing it with no badge or Fast Dash. In order to get the most height, you must input your next bubble *after* bouncing on the previous bubble, but this is risky and is where bubble RNG is most lethal.

#### Bubble Ladder

A bubble ladder is very similar in concept to an IBJ, however the purpose is to gain much more vertical distance than horizontal. This can be done using the **Boosting Spin Jump** badge, and is utilized during the [Bowser fight to save a lot of time](https://notcodek.github.io/TestProjectWonderTut/bowser).

The inputs are exactly the same as an IBJ except you want to *alternate your direction after each bubble jump.*
