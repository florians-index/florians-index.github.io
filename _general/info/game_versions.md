# Version Differences

This page has a lot of assorted information on differences between updates, console versions, physical vs. digital, and languages.

## Update Versions

SMBW has been updated numerous times since it's release on October 20th 2023, but *most* have been insignificant.

There is a ***massive*** exception to this, however, which is the changes made from **v1.0.0 to v1.0.1**

Version 1.0.1 and onward have *patched out the Overworld Out-of-Bound exploits that are utilized in the Any% category.*

This means that, unfortunately, ***Any% can ONLY be run on v1.0.0, severely impacting the accessibility of the category.***

If you wish to run Any%, you will need to keep v1.0.0 at all costs. *If you have a physical copy, you are able to downgrade by formatting your system.* Just make sure to back-up your save data! If you own the game digitally, you are unfortunately out of luck **without the use of a modded Switch.**

For most categories like *Any% No Major Glitches* and *100%*, the update version has little-to-no impact on the gameplay.

## Switch 1 vs. Switch 2

SMBW can be played on Switch 1 or Switch 2, and the only differences between the two are... ***load times!***

Switch 2 saves **~45s in No Major Glitches, and ~2m in 100%** over Switch 1. This makes a *massive* difference at top level, while matters less at lower levels. If you wish to compete seriously in these categories, Switch 2 is required for all intents and purposes.

Another benefit of playing on Switch 2 is that you don't have to worry about **setting up your intro load**. On Switch 1, you can *save 1s by starting a new game, waiting for the intro to load, closing the game through the Home Menu, and then opening the game again to start your run.* This is obviously a hassle (especially if you are using the optimal language set-up), but thankfully this is **unnecessary on Switch 2**.

It is important to note that when we are comparing Switch 1 vs. Switch 2, we are doing so using the ***Switch 1 Edition on Switch 2***, which is important to mention due to Switch 2 Edition's changes...

#### Switch 2 Edition
On March 26th 2026, the Switch 2 Edition (S2E) of SMBW was released, which added new content and improvements for casual players.

For starters, we have S2E exclusive categories on speedrun.com, including:
- [All Koopalings](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=all-koopalings&x=q25l9ryk) (defeat every Koopaling)
- [Concert Ending](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=concert-ending&x=jdr38zgd) (reach the credits after defeating every Koopaling + collecting every instrument)
- [100% Switch 2 Edition](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw?h=100-switch-2-edition-decorations&x=02q175jd-0nwdmp08.qj7rm4eq) (normal 100% requirements + completing Bellabel Park, with variables to include Decorations or not)
- Toad Brigade Missions and Koopaling fights on [our IL leaderboard](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw/levels)

As for the main 3 [Switch 1 Edition categories](https://notcodek.github.io/TestProjectWonderTut/categories)...
- **Any% is impossible** due to Overworld Out-of-Bounds being patched out
- Any% No Major Glitches *can be done*, however is much slower to do so, as **some cutscenes have added voice acting that cannot be mashed through, along with a forced Koopaling encounter in World 1**
- 100% is in the same boat for similar reasons as NMG, however the issue is even worse as **Rosalina adds an extra 12 standees to the count, making the run even more unforgiving**

As such, it is ***not recommended to use Switch 2 Edition for the Switch 1 Edition categories.***

Thankfully, if you have it installed and want to run NMG or S1E 100%, *you can freely swap back to Switch 1 Edition on the main menu of Switch 2 Edition.*

## Physical vs. Digital

Since SMBW is a Switch title, that means you can play on a physical cartridge through the card slot, or a digital download on your internal storage or SD card.

To keep this brief, **digital is faster than physical due to load times.** However, *we have no idea by how much.*

It is estimated that Digital saves around ~10s over Physical in **100% on Switch 1**, but we don't know for sure.

That being said, the difference is negligible enough to the point where you should not stress if you play the game physically.

## Language

Since the Switch has support for a multitude of different languages, that means you can swap to any language regardless of region. Skip to the TL;DR if you just want the optimal languages.

The most optimal language for ***TEXT*** is **Spanish**, as it has the least amount of textboxes that need to be mashed for, saving less than a second. This is a *very* minimal timesave, but if you want to take advantage of it, **you need to change your System Language.** Both Latin America and Spain versions are the same, as far as we currently know. Keep in mind if you're playing *Any% or 100%, Taily's Toxic Pond's Quiz will be in your System Language as well, making it hard to navigate.*

Now, interestingly, this is not where this discussion ends! SMBW gives you to the option to *change the voice language of the Talking Flower dialogue throughout the game.*

This might seem insignificant, however, **the length of Taking Flower voicelines impact the length of some cutscenes, such as the World Completed cutscenes.**

That means there is a second optimal language, being **Simplified Chinese for Talking Flower voices, which saves about ~2s**. In order to take advantage of this time save, you must follow these steps before the beginning of each run...

- Start a New Game
- Pause to open the settings
- Scroll to the Talking Flower language option (2nd from the bottom)
- Change the language to Simplified Chinese (2 to the left of default)
- Close the settings and then close the game with the home menu
- Start your run!

This is quite tedious, especially for a reset-heavy runner, so we do not recommend using this timesave unless you are at a top level, or don't mind the extra set-up time.

***TL;DR*** - The fastest languages are Spanish for text (System Language), and Simplified Chinese
