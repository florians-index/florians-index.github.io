# Taily's Toxic Pond Quiz Q&A

In the level *Taily's Toxic Pond*, the Wonder Section is in the form of a quiz game show, where you are asked questions and must answer by defeating one of the three colored Taily's that contains the correct answer.

You must correctly answer ***3*** questions in order to be awarded a Wonder Seed, and these questions are picked randomly from a large pool of questions.

You can find the questions and their corresponding answers in the table below!

<details>
<summary>How many Goombas have you defeated?</summary>

The Goomba question is notorious for annoying runners as it is the only question with an unpredictable answer. Unless you keep a tally throughout the run (which is quite annoying), you will pretty much have to guess. In the **100%** category, you typically want to guess the number closest to ***30***

</details>

<br>

<details>
<summary>How many standees do you have?</summary>

The Standee question is easy in **Any%**, being ***12*** as that is the default number. In **100%**, however, your Standee count is different in every attempt. This is why you want to have a LiveSplit counter component to keep track of your current Standee count, as it lets you answer this question with ease, as long as having info for flower coin calculations.

</details>
