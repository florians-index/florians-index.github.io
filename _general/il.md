# Individual Levels

This page is for those interested in running **Individual Levels, or ILs**.

You can find the leaderboard for all of our ILs [here](https://www.speedrun.com/smbw/levels)!

## Restrictions

We have a list of important restrictions you should keep in mind if you plan to submit to our IL leaderboard:
- Runs must have the exit goal already completed prior to the attempt.
- The whole IL must be shown in one video segment. ILs may not be spliced together.
- All runs must **start with a reset from the pause menu**. IGT levels are an exception.
- Multiplayer is banned.
- The use of online features is banned.
- Assist mode is banned.
- 100% ILs require the top of the flagpole only once. Normal and secret exits are needed, but not fake exits.
- Resetting levels for the intention of changing the position of upcoming obstacles (i.e. pipes and Wonder Seeds) is banned.
- All characters are allowed.
- All badges are allowed.
- All items are allowed, including reserved items.

## Timing

**For levels that use an In-Game Timer (IGT), which include Wiggler Races and KO Arenas, IGT is used instead of RTA.**

For the rest of the levels, here is how to retime your ILs:

#### Start Frame
First frame when the loading circle has completely vanished.

#### End Frame
First frame when the screen has turned black from the loading circle after completing a level.

For example, this is the frame **before** timing ends:

![]({{ '/assets/images/ilendbefore.png' | relative_url }})

(Notice the *extremely* tiny circle still present on the center/bottom right. Timing ends with the circle is completely gone)

This is the correct end frame:

![]({{ '/assets/images/ilendcorrect.png' | relative_url }})

***This is why it is very important to record your ILs in 60fps if possible, so they can be retimed properly. 30fps can make it harder/impossible to tell the proper start and end frame.***

For *Bowser's Rage Stage*, the final frame is the same as full-game categories: **first frame of the button press animation**.
